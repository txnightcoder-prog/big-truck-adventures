Learn Every Day
Daily Activities for Kids
Fun things to do with your child after watching each episode — 5 minutes or less!
After Episode 1
Jensen's Letter Hunt

Walk around your home and find objects that start with the letter P — Pillow, Pot, Pan, Picture! Then find things that start with J!

Skills: Letters P & J  |  Ages 3–5
After Episode 2
Alphabet Truck Parade

Write A–Z on small pieces of paper. Let your child arrange them in order like the trucks in the parade. Celebrate each letter they get right!

Skills: Full Alphabet  |  Ages 3–6
After Episode 3
Cone Counting Walk

On your next walk, count objects you pass — mailboxes, trees, steps, cars. Count all the way to 20 just like Jensen counted the cones!

Skills: Counting 1–20  |  Ages 2–5
After Episode 4
Shape Sign Safari

Drive or walk and spot road signs! Ask your child: "What shape is that?" Triangle, square, circle, rectangle — just like Bentley's road trip!

Skills: Shapes  |  Ages 3–6
After Episode 5
Russell's Mystery Object

Put a toy in a bag. Give your child 3 clues — color, shape, what it does. Can they guess it like Russell guessed the excavator? Take turns!

Skills: Colors, Shapes, Thinking  |  Ages 3–6
After Episode 6
Days of the Week Song

Sing the days of the week every morning at breakfast: "Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday!" Ask: what day is today?

Skills: Days of Week  |  Ages 2–5
After Episode 7 — Season Finale!
Emery's Rainbow Surprise

Make a rainbow craft together! Color or paint the 6 rainbow colors in order: Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Purple. Give it to someone you love — just like Emery's big surprise for Mom!

Skills: Rainbow Colors, Feelings, Sharing Love  |  Ages 2–6
For Parents & Caregivers
Help & Parent Guide
Everything you need to know about Big Truck Adventures.
What Will My Child Learn?
  • Letters & Alphabet — A through Z, letter sounds, letter recognition
  • Numbers & Counting — counting 1–20, number recognition
  • Colors & Shapes — basic colors, 5 core shapes, rainbow colors
  • Days of the Week — Monday through Sunday, sequencing
  • Social Skills — teamwork, kindness, helping others, family love
  • Critical Thinking — following directions, solving problems with clues
Is This Safe for My Child?
  • All episodes are designed for children ages 2–6
  • Content is 100% wholesome — no scary moments, no conflict
  • The channel is set as YouTube Kids eligible (Made for Kids)
  • No comments enabled on videos — kids-safe viewing
  • Episodes feature family values — siblings, teamwork, kindness to babies
  • Each episode ends with an interactive counting or naming moment
