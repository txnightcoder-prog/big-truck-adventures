# BIG TRUCK ADVENTURES
## Character Art Briefs — Visual Reference Guide

---

## BENTLEY — Age 6 | The Leader & Baseball Star

**Physical Description:**
- Blonde hair, neatly combed but always slightly messed up from being active
- Bright blue eyes, confident expression
- Tall and sturdy for a 6-year-old — looks like a natural leader
- Default outfit: Baseball jersey (blue & white), baseball cap worn forward, jeans, sneakers
- Always has his baseball somewhere — in his hand, tucked under his arm, or in his back pocket

**Facial Expressions (key poses needed):**
- Confident smirk — "I have a plan"
- Proud big-brother smile — arms crossed, nodding
- Focused game-face — eyes narrowed, ready to go
- Surprised/flustered — when Russell does something unpredictable

**Truck Friend: Max the Dump Truck**
- Max is big, sturdy, blue — matches Bentley's energy
- Max's face (on the grille) is calm and dependable

---

## JENSEN — Age 4 | The Future Kindergartener

**Physical Description:**
- Light brown hair, slightly wavy, a little messy — like he just ran through the yard
- Tall and skinny for a 4-year-old — long legs, lanky arms
- Very expressive face — his emotions show immediately (widest eyes, biggest gasps, biggest grins)
- Default outfit: Colorful t-shirt (yellow or orange), shorts, light-up sneakers
- Almost always wearing or carrying his backpack — it's slightly too big for him
- Signature look: eyebrows raised high when he's excited or figuring something out

**Facial Expressions (key poses needed):**
- Eyes wide, mouth open — "I just figured it out!"
- Proud to camera — finger-guns or thumbs up — "I learned that for kindergarten!"
- Confused squint — holding paper upside down
- Laughing — full body laugh, head thrown back

**Truck Friend: Charlie the Crane Truck**
- Charlie is amber/yellow, tall and elegant like a crane
- Charlie's face is wise and warm — like a kind teacher

---

## RUSSELL — Age 3 | The Truck Expert & Curious Explorer

**Physical Description:**
- Blonde hair, fine and a little fluffy — sticks up slightly at the top
- Blue eyes — wide and always looking around at everything
- Small and compact — the shortest of the brothers
- Round glasses (signature look) — slightly too big for his face, always sliding down his nose
- Default outfit: Green t-shirt with a truck on it, cargo shorts with lots of pockets, chunky sneakers
- Often caught mid-run or mid-jump — rarely standing still

**Facial Expressions (key poses needed):**
- Excited pointing — "VROOM VROOM! That's a [truck name]!"
- Glasses sliding down nose — pushing them back up with one finger
- Confidently wrong face — big smile, totally sure of himself even when incorrect
- Caught-in-the-act look — freeze, wide eyes, slow grin

**Truck Friend: Remy the Road Roller**
- Remy is green, round, and sturdy — matches Russell's compact energy
- Remy's face is cheerful and a little goofy — always happy

---

## EMERY — Age 8 Months | The Baby & Heart of the Family

**Physical Description:**
- Blonde hair — very fine, soft wisps on top, little curl at the front
- Big round eyes — warm and full of wonder (eye color: blue/grey, still developing)
- Chubby baby cheeks, tiny button nose, always has a big open-mouth smile or a look of pure wonder
- Default outfit: Pink onesie with a tiny truck or flower on it, pink bow in her hair
- Always in her **walker** — a round baby walker with colorful toys on the tray
- Tiny chubby hands that grab at everything within reach

**Facial Expressions (key poses needed):**
- Pure joy giggle — arms up, mouth wide open
- Concentrating — leaning forward in walker, grabbing for something
- Surprised delight — eyes huge, little gasp
- Sleepy — head drooping, eyes half closed, still smiling

**Truck Friend: Bella the Baby Loader**
- Bella is small, pink, and gentle — moves slowly and carefully
- Bella's face is sweet and shy — big eyes, soft smile
- Bella is the same scale as Emery's walker — they're always side by side

---

## MOM

**Physical Description:**
- Warm, kind face — always smiling or laughing at the kids
- Blonde hair (the kids get their blonde from her), shoulder length
- Casual and practical outfit — jeans, colorful top, often has a dish towel or coffee mug in hand
- Appears in kitchen scenes, doorway watching moments, and town scenes with Emery
