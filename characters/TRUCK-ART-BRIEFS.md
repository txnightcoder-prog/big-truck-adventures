# BIG TRUCK ADVENTURES
## Truck Character Art Briefs — Visual Reference Guide

---

## MAX — Bentley's Dump Truck

**Personality:** Strong, dependable, calm under pressure — the big brother of the truck crew. Never panics. Always ready.

**Physical Design:**
- **Type:** Full-size dump truck (largest truck in the crew)
- **Primary color:** Deep blue (matches Bentley's jersey)
- **Secondary color:** Silver/chrome on the dump bed edges and bumper
- **Size:** Biggest truck — towers over Charlie and Remy
- **Dump bed:** Always slightly tilted back, like he's ready to work
- **Tires:** Extra wide and chunky — leaves big tire tracks

**Face (on the grille):**
- Strong jaw, square face shape
- Calm, steady eyes — half-lidded (relaxed confidence)
- Slight smirk — like he's seen it all before
- Eyebrows: flat and firm, rarely raised
- Voice feel: Deep, slow, rumbling — like the truck engine itself

**Key Poses:**
- Ready stance — dump bed raised slightly, engine revving
- Carrying a full load — bed piled high, focused expression
- Proud moment — when Bentley gets something right, Max gives a slow nod

**Catchphrase:** *"Load's ready. Let's roll."*

---

## CHARLIE — Jensen's Crane Truck

**Personality:** Warm, wise, encouraging — like a favorite teacher. Patient with everyone, especially Russell. Always believes in Jensen.

**Physical Design:**
- **Type:** Mobile crane truck (tallest truck in the crew)
- **Primary color:** Amber/golden yellow (warm and inviting)
- **Secondary color:** Cream/off-white on the cab and crane arm
- **Size:** Tall and elegant — the crane arm extends high above everything
- **Crane arm:** Always slightly extended, like he's ready to help lift something
- **Hook:** A shiny golden hook at the end of the crane line

**Face (on the cab front):**
- Round, soft face shape — gentle and approachable
- Warm eyes — wide and bright, like they're always interested
- Gentle smile — always present, never forced
- Small, neat eyebrows — raised slightly (curious and kind)
- Voice feel: Warm, measured, encouraging — like a patient teacher

**Key Poses:**
- Listening pose — crane arm lowered, eyes on whoever is talking
- Helping pose — crane arm extended, hook lowering to assist
- Proud teacher moment — when Jensen solves a problem, Charlie beams

**Catchphrase:** *"I knew you could figure it out!"*

---

## REMY — Russell's Road Roller

**Personality:** Cheerful, goofy, enthusiastic — matches Russell's energy perfectly. Always happy, always moving (even if slowly). The fun one.

**Physical Design:**
- **Type:** Road roller / steamroller (rounder shape than the others)
- **Primary color:** Bright green (matches Russell's shirt)
- **Secondary color:** Yellow-green on the roller drums
- **Size:** Short and wide — compact like Russell, lower to the ground
- **Rollers:** Two big round drums — front and back — always slightly spinning
- **Shape:** Rounder and chubbier than the other trucks — like a happy little tank

**Face (on the front cab):**
- Round, chubby face shape — widest of all the trucks
- Big, wide eyes — always looking around with excitement
- Big open grin — teeth showing, pure joy
- Eyebrows: always raised high (permanently excited)
- Voice feel: High-energy, slightly squeaky, enthusiastic — like Russell in truck form

**Key Poses:**
- Zooming pose — rollers spinning fast, leaning forward
- Surprised pose — eyes huge, rollers locked (skidding to a stop)
- Sitting-still pose — clearly struggling to hold still, wheels twitching

**Catchphrase:** *"ROLL WITH IT!"*

---

## BELLA — Emery's Baby Loader

**Personality:** Sweet, shy, gentle — the quietest of the truck crew. Moves carefully and slowly. Deeply loyal to Emery. Often accidentally carries the most important thing in her bucket.

**Physical Design:**
- **Type:** Small front loader / baby loader (smallest truck in the crew)
- **Primary color:** Soft pink (matches Emery's onesie)
- **Secondary color:** White on the loader bucket and cab roof
- **Size:** Smallest truck — same scale as Emery's walker, always beside her
- **Loader bucket:** Small, round, and always slightly full — she's always carrying something
- **Movement:** Slow and careful — putters rather than zooms

**Face (on the front):**
- Small, delicate face shape
- Big, round eyes — soft and gentle, like a baby deer
- Shy smile — small and sweet, one side slightly higher
- Eyebrows: soft and slightly furrowed (always a little concerned but trying her best)
- Voice feel: Soft, quiet, little beeps — no words, just gentle sounds (like Emery)

**Key Poses:**
- Beside Emery's walker — always within arm's reach of Emery
- Carrying something important — bucket full, moving carefully
- Surprised — bucket drops whatever she's holding at the exact right moment

**Catchphrase:** *(soft beep)* — she communicates only in gentle beeps, matching Emery's "Ba-ba-BRRM!"

## TRUCK RELATIONSHIPS

- **Max & Bentley** — best friends, same confident energy, Max executes Bentley's plans without question
- **Charlie & Jensen** — teacher/student dynamic, Charlie never tells Jensen the answer — always guides him to find it himself
- **Remy & Russell** — partners in chaos, they egg each other on, both always moving
- **Bella & Emery** — inseparable, Bella stays at walker-height so Emery can always reach her, they communicate in matching sounds
