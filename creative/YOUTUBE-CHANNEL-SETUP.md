## STEP 4: MADE FOR KIDS SETTING

**This is critical for appearing in YouTube Kids and complying with COPPA.**

1. Go to **YouTube Studio**
2. Click **Settings** → **Channel** → **Advanced Settings**
3. Under **Audience** → select **"Yes, set this channel as made for kids"**
4. Click **Save**

Also set it per video when uploading:
- In the upload flow → **Audience** → "Yes, this video is made for kids"

> ⚠️ Note: "Made for Kids" videos cannot use personalized ads, comments are disabled,
> and some features like end screens linking to external sites are restricted.
> This is the right setting for a kids show — it unlocks YouTube Kids placement.
