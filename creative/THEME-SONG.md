# BIG TRUCK ADVENTURES
## Theme Song — "Big Truck Adventures"
### Intro Jingle (Full Version + Short Version)

---

## FULL THEME SONG (0:00–0:30)
*Played at the start of every episode*
*Tone: Upbeat, energetic, singable — think early Blippi / Cocomelon energy but with more personality*
*Tempo: Fast and bouncy — 120 BPM*
*Instruments: Electric guitar riff, drums, clapping, truck horn accents*

---

### VERSE 1
*(Bentley's voice — confident, leading)*
> We wake up every morning, ready for the day,
> Grab our trucks and helmets, then we're on our way!
> Max is revving up and Charlie's swinging high,
> Remy's rolling fast beneath the bright blue sky!

### CHORUS
*(All voices together — big and bold)*
> BIG TRUCK ADVENTURES, here we GO! *(truck horn: BEEP BEEP!)*
> Letters and numbers, put on a SHOW!
> Bentley, Jensen, Russell — let's RIDE!
> Baby Emery's along for the VROOM-VROOM VROOM VROOM ride! *(Emery: Ba-ba-BRRM!)*

> BIG TRUCK ADVENTURES, can you SEE?
> Every day's a mystery for you and ME!
> Count it out and spell it right —
> Big Truck Adventures from morning to NIGHT!

### VERSE 2
*(Jensen's voice — excited, building)*
> Jensen's got his backpack and his thinking cap on tight,
> Gonna use his letters and his numbers just right!
> Russell's on the lookout — he knows every truck!
> *(Russell interrupts: "That's a FRUCK— I mean a TRUCK!")*
> *(everyone laughs)*

### BRIDGE
*(Slower, each character gets a line — over fun visuals)*
> **BENTLEY:** I lead the team and we don't stop!
> **JENSEN:** I count and spell right to the top!
> **RUSSELL:** I know my trucks from A to Z!
> **EMERY:** *(happy baby babble)* Ba-ba-BRRM!
> **ALL:** That's our crew — come roll with us, you'll SEE!

### FINAL CHORUS
*(Biggest version — full energy, all instruments in)*
> BIG TRUCK ADVENTURES, here we GO! *(BEEP BEEP!)*
> Letters and numbers, put on a SHOW!
> Bentley, Jensen, Russell — let's RIDE!
> Baby Emery's along for the VROOM-VROOM VROOM VROOM ride!

> BIG TRUCK ADVENTURES — roll all DAY!
> Learning something new in every single WAY!
> Trucks and letters, friends and FUN —
> Big Truck Adventures has just BEGUN!

### OUTRO STING
*(Short 4-beat hit — used at end of show too)*
> *(BEEP BEEP — engine rev — Emery giggles)*
> "Big Truck... ADVENTURES!"

---

## SHORT VERSION (0:00–0:08)

> BIG TRUCK ADVENTURES, here we GO! *(BEEP BEEP!)*
> Bentley, Jensen, Russell — let's RIDE!
> Baby Emery: Ba-ba-BRRM!
> *(engine rev + drum hit)*

## OUTRO SONG (end of every episode, 0:00–0:15)
*Slower, warm, winding down*

> We learned something new today, me and you,
> Letters, numbers, colors — we worked it through!
> See you next time — can't wait to explore!
> Big Truck Adventures... there's always MORE!
> *(soft truck horn, Emery giggle, music fades)*

## PRODUCTION NOTES

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Tempo | 120 BPM — fast and bouncy |
| Key | C major (bright and happy) |
| Style reference | Early Blippi theme / Cocomelon energy / slightly more rock-influenced |
| Instruments | Electric guitar lead, drums, handclaps, bass, truck horn sound effects |
| Voices | 4 character voices + group sections |
| Emery's part | Always just "Ba-ba-BRRM!" — but placed perfectly for laughs |
| Russell's running gag | "That's a FRUCK— I mean a TRUCK!" appears in Verse 2 |
| Viewer hook | Clapping rhythm in chorus encourages kids to clap along |
| Earworm design | "VROOM-VROOM VROOM VROOM ride" — repetition makes it stick |
| Episode use | Full version: E01 / Short version: any episode with cold open |
| Tools to create | Suno AI, Udio, or commission a children's music composer |
