# BIG TRUCK ADVENTURES
## Episode 2: "The Alphabet Truck Parade"
**Runtime:** ~7 minutes | **Educational Focus:** Alphabet A–Z, Letter Sounds, Ordering
**Characters:** Bentley, Jensen, Russell, Emery, Mom, Charlie, Max, Remy, Bella + 26 Alphabet Trucks

---

## SCENE 1 — INTRO SONG (0:00–0:30)

*[UPBEAT THEME MUSIC — truck engine revs, horn honks]*

**ALL CHARACTERS (singing):**
> Big Truck Adventures, here we go!
> Letters and numbers, let's put on a show!
> Bentley, Jensen, Russell, and Emery too —
> Big Truck Adventures — just me and you!

*[Trucks drive across screen. Emery bounces in her walker. "Ba-ba-BRRM!"]*

---

## SCENE 2 — THE TOWN SQUARE (0:30–1:15)

*[Sunny morning. The whole town is decorated with colorful banners and flags. Jensen, Bentley, and Russell stand in the town square. Emery zooms around in her walker. Mom watches from a bench.]*

**JENSEN:** *(spinning around looking at all the decorations)* Whoa... WHOA... WHOAAA!

**BENTLEY:** Jensen, close your mouth. A bug will fly in.

**JENSEN:** *(snaps mouth shut, then immediately opens it again)* But BENTLEY — it's the Annual Truck Town PARADE DAY!

**RUSSELL:** *(jumping up and down)* PARADE! PARADE! PARADE!

**EMERY:** *(slamming walker tray excitedly)* Ba-ba-BRRM!

*[Charlie the Crane Truck rolls up, looking worried.]*

**CHARLIE:** Oh good, you're all here! We have a big problem. The 26 Alphabet Trucks are all here for the parade — but they're completely out of order!

**BENTLEY:** Out of order?

**CHARLIE:** Every truck has a letter — A through Z. They have to march in the right order or the town's big surprise won't unlock!

**JENSEN:** *(gasps)* What's the surprise?!

**CHARLIE:** Nobody knows! It only unlocks when ALL 26 letters are in the right order. A to Z!

**RUSSELL:** *(very serious, glasses sliding down)* I know ALL the letters. A... B... C... *(long pause)* ...truck.

**BENTLEY:** *(sighs)* That's not a letter, Russell.

**JENSEN:** I can do it! I've been practicing my alphabet for kindergarten! Let's go find the trucks!

---

## SCENE 3 — FINDING TRUCKS A THROUGH F (1:15–2:30)

*[The team splits up to find the out-of-order trucks scattered around town.]*

### TRUCK A — THE DUMP TRUCK
*[A big red truck with a giant letter "A" painted on the side is parked outside the bakery.]*

**JENSEN:** There's the A truck! A is first — A always goes first!

**RUSSELL:** A for... APPLE!

**JENSEN:** That's right Russell! A makes the "ahh" sound — Aaa-Apple! Aaa-Adventure!

*[Jensen waves the A truck into position at the front of the parade line.]*

### TRUCK B — THE BULLDOZER
*[A yellow bulldozer with a "B" is pushing dirt around in the park.]*

**BENTLEY:** B truck — that's second! B comes right after A!

**JENSEN:** B makes the "buh" sound — Buh-Bentley! Buh-Baseball!

**BENTLEY:** *(pleased)* Hey, that's me!

### TRUCKS C, D, E, F
*[Quick montage — Jensen spots each truck, calls out the letter sound and a word.]*

**JENSEN:** C is for Charlie! *(Charlie beeps proudly)*
D is for Dump Truck! *(a tiny dump truck waves)*
E is for... E is for... Emery!

*[Emery, who has somehow rolled her walker right into the parade staging area, waves her arms.]*

**MOM:** *(calling from afar)* Emery! How did you get over there?!

**EMERY:** *(proud baby smile)*

**JENSEN:** F is for... *(looks at Russell)*

**RUSSELL:** *(confidently)* F is for... FRUCK!

**BENTLEY:** That's not a word.

**RUSSELL:** TRUCK. With an F.

**JENSEN:** F is for FRIENDS! *(points at everyone)* That's us!

---

## SCENE 4 — THE TRICKY LETTERS (2:30–4:00)

*[Jensen has A through M lined up. Now things get harder.]*

**JENSEN:** Okay... N, O, P, Q... *(counts on fingers)* These are the tricky ones in the middle!

**CHARLIE:** You've got this, Jensen. What sound does N make?

**JENSEN:** N makes the "nnn" sound — Nnn-Nine! Nnn-Night!

*[He finds the N truck — a night-construction truck with floodlights — and waves it into place.]*

**BENTLEY:** *(spotting a truck parked sideways)* There's the O truck! O is for... 

**JENSEN:** O makes the "oh" sound — Oh! Like when you're surprised! OH! And O is for Orange... and O is for... *(looks around)* ...O is for OVAL! Like that truck's tires!

**RUSSELL:** *(peering at a truck with a Q on it)* Q... Q... *(pushes glasses up)* Q is weird. Q looks like an O that sat on something.

**JENSEN:** *(laughing)* Q makes the "kwuh" sound — Kwuh-Queen! Kwuh-Quiet! *(whispers)* Quiet, Russell.

**RUSSELL:** *(whispers back)* I'm being KWUH-iet!

*[Jensen lines up N through R. Now he hits a snag.]*

**JENSEN:** *(staring at two trucks)* Wait... which comes first — S or T?

**BENTLEY:** *(thinking)* S... T... S comes before T!

**JENSEN:** *(sings softly)* A-B-C-D-E-F-G... H-I-J-K-L-M-N-O-P... Q-R-S... *(lights up)* S! S comes before T!

**CHARLIE:** That's exactly right! Singing the alphabet helps you find the order!

**JENSEN:** I learned that for kindergarten!

---

## SCENE 5 — THE FINAL STRETCH (4:00–5:15)

*[Jensen is on a roll — U through X lined up. Only Y and Z remain. But the Y truck is missing.]*

**BENTLEY:** Where's the Y truck?

**RUSSELL:** *(pointing dramatically)* I FOUND IT!

*[The Y truck — a bright yellow yardwork truck — is parked behind the ice cream shop. Russell is sitting on its bumper eating an ice cream cone.]*

**BENTLEY:** Russell! How long have you been back there?!

**RUSSELL:** *(mouth full of ice cream)* Y is for Yummy.

**JENSEN:** *(laughing)* Y IS for Yummy! Y makes the "yuh" sound — Yuh-Yellow! Yuh-Yummy! Yuh-YES!

*[Jensen guides the Y truck into second-to-last position.]*

**JENSEN:** Now Z! Z is LAST — Z is always last!

**RUSSELL:** *(ice cream gone)* Where's the Z truck?

*[Everyone looks around. No Z truck anywhere. The team is stumped.]*

**CHARLIE:** Oh no... without the Z truck, the parade can't finish and the surprise won't unlock!

*[A small rumbling sound. Everyone looks down. Emery in her walker is slowly but surely pushing the tiniest truck anyone has ever seen — a small zebra-striped street sweeper with a big letter Z on the side. She found it under a bench.]*

**JENSEN:** EMERY! She found the Z truck!

**BENTLEY:** How does she always do that?

**EMERY:** *(triumphant baby noise)* Ba-ba-BRRM!

**MOM:** *(laughing)* That's my girl.

---

## SCENE 6 — THE PARADE & THE SURPRISE (5:15–6:30)

*[All 26 Alphabet Trucks line up in perfect A-to-Z order. The town square fills with people. Jensen stands at the front, beaming.]*

**CHARLIE:** Jensen — would you like to do the honors?

**JENSEN:** *(to the parade)* Alphabet Trucks... MARCH!

*[The trucks roll forward one by one. As each passes, Jensen calls out the letter and a word.]*

**JENSEN:** A for Adventure! B for Bentley! C for Charlie! D for Determined!

**RUSSELL:** E for Excellent! *(runs alongside)* F for... FRUCK— I mean, FRIENDS!

**BENTLEY:** G for Go team! H for Here we go!

*[The last truck — Emery's Z truck — rolls past. A giant golden KEY falls from the sky and lands in the town square. It glows.]*

**ALL:** Whoa...

*[Jensen picks up the key. He walks to a giant box in the center of the square. He puts the key in. The box opens — and out pops a massive banner that reads:*
**"CONGRATULATIONS JENSEN — ALPHABET CHAMPION!"**
*Confetti, horns, the trucks all honk at once.]*

**JENSEN:** *(to camera, eyes wide)* The surprise... was FOR ME?!

**BENTLEY:** *(grinning)* We may have set that up.

**RUSSELL:** I helped! I ate the ice cream as a distraction!

**BENTLEY:** That... was not part of the plan.

*[Emery claps. Mom hugs Jensen.]*

---

## SCENE 7 — JENSEN'S MOMENT (6:30–6:50)

**JENSEN:** *(to camera)*
I learned SO MUCH today for kindergarten!
The alphabet goes A to Z — TWENTY-SIX letters!
Every letter makes a special sound —
A says "aah", B says "buh", C says "kuh"...
And if you ever forget the order? Just SING IT!
*(sings)* A-B-C-D-E-F-G!

**RUSSELL:** *(jumping in)* H-I-J-K-ELEMENOPEE!

**JENSEN:** *(laughing)* It's L-M-N-O-P, Russell. Five separate letters!

**RUSSELL:** *(shrugs)* I like my version.

---

## SCENE 8 — OUTRO (6:50–7:00)

*[All characters wave. Trucks honk. Confetti still falling. Emery's walker has confetti stuck to it.]*

**JENSEN:** *(to camera)* Hey friends — can YOU sing the alphabet? Sing it with me next time!

**ALL:** See you next time on Big Truck Adventures!

**RUSSELL:** VROOM VROOM!

**EMERY:** Ba-ba-BRRM!

*[THEME MUSIC plays out. Subscribe animation appears.]*
