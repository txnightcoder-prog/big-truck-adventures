# BIG TRUCK ADVENTURES
## Episode 7: "Emery's Big Surprise"
**Runtime:** ~7 minutes | **Educational Focus:** Family & Love, Colors, Feelings/Emotions, Helping Others
**Characters:** Bentley, Jensen, Russell, Emery, Mom, Max, Charlie, Remy, Bella, Dig, Foreman Bear

---

## SCENE 1 — INTRO SONG (0:00–0:30)
*[Full theme song — ALL trucks including Dig now in the lineup]*

---

## SCENE 2 — THE PLAN (0:30–1:15)

*[Early morning. Mom is still asleep. Bentley, Jensen, and Russell are huddled in the kitchen whispering. Emery is in her walker, watching them with huge curious eyes.]*

**BENTLEY:** *(whispering)* Okay. Today is Mom's special day. We're going to set up the BEST surprise ever in the backyard. Decorations, her favorite flowers, and a banner that says we love her.

**JENSEN:** *(whispering, very excited, barely containing volume)* I can make the banner! I'll write the letters!

**RUSSELL:** *(whispering so loud it's basically talking)* I'll get the TRUCKS to help with the decorations!

**BENTLEY:** *(covers Russell's mouth)* Shhhh!

*[Emery watches all of this. She looks at the door where Mom is sleeping. She looks at her brothers. She bounces once in her walker — very determined.]*

**BENTLEY:** And Emery... *(looks at her)* ...you just stay here, okay? Don't ruin the surprise.

**EMERY:** *(angelic smile)*

*[The brothers head outside. Emery watches them go. Then she slowly, deliberately begins rolling her walker toward the backyard.]*

---

## SCENE 3 — THE SETUP (1:15–2:30)

*[The backyard. Max is carrying a big pile of colorful decorations. Charlie is lifting a banner. Remy is smoothing out a flat area for the celebration. Bella putters around tidying things up. Dig has carefully dug small holes for flower pots.]*

**BENTLEY:** *(directing)* Charlie — hang the banner right there! Max — put the flowers in Dig's pots! Remy — smooth out that patch for the table!

*[Everything is going perfectly. Then — a tiny BUMP sound. Everyone freezes.]*

*[Emery has rolled her walker right into the middle of the backyard. She looks around at all the decorations, completely delighted. She grabs a streamer with one chubby hand and waves it.]*

**RUSSELL:** EMERY!

**BENTLEY:** *(panicked whisper)* She got out!

**JENSEN:** How did she get through the door?!

*[Everyone looks at the door. Bella is standing right next to it. The door is slightly ajar. Bella looks innocent.]*

**RUSSELL:** ...Bella let her out.

**BELLA:** *(tiny apologetic beep)*

---

## SCENE 4 — EMERY HELPS (2:30–4:00)

*[Every time the brothers try to set something up, Emery rolls into the middle of it. But every time — accidentally — she makes it BETTER.]*

### DECORATION 1 — THE FLOWERS

*[Jensen is carefully sorting flowers by color — red, yellow, pink, purple — to put in the pots.]*

**JENSEN:** Red flowers here... yellow flowers here... pink flowers—

*[Emery reaches out from her walker and grabs a bunch of ALL FOUR colors at once and drops them into one pot together.]*

**JENSEN:** Emery! I was sorting by—  *(looks at the pot)* ...actually that looks really pretty.

*[The mixed-color pot IS beautiful — a rainbow of flowers.]*

**JENSEN:** *(to camera)* Red, yellow, pink, AND purple — all four colors together make a RAINBOW bouquet!

### DECORATION 2 — THE BANNER

*[Bentley is trying to hang the banner — "WE LOVE YOU MOM" — but it keeps drooping on one side.]*

**BENTLEY:** It needs something to hold up this corner...

*[Emery rolls over and grabs the drooping corner with her fist and holds it up, giggling.]*

**BENTLEY:** *(stares at her)* ...Are you holding the banner?

*[Emery beams.]*

**BENTLEY:** *(softly)* ...That actually works. Thanks, Emery.

### DECORATION 3 — THE SURPRISE PATH

*[Russell is laying out a path of colored stepping stones from the back door to the celebration table. But he's put them in the wrong color order.]*

**RUSSELL:** *(standing back)* There. Perfect.

**JENSEN:** Russell... those are not in any order.

**RUSSELL:** They're in MY order.

*[Emery slowly rolls her walker along the path — and wherever she stops, she picks up a stone and moves it. When she's done, the path goes: RED, ORANGE, YELLOW, GREEN, BLUE, PURPLE — a perfect rainbow order.]*

**EVERYONE:** ...

**RUSSELL:** *(slowly)* She... made a rainbow.

**JENSEN:** Emery sorted the colors! RED, ORANGE, YELLOW, GREEN, BLUE, PURPLE — rainbow order!

**EMERY:** *(proud baby noise)*

---

## SCENE 5 — MOM WAKES UP (4:00–5:00)

*[The brothers hear Mom stirring inside. Everyone panics — last-minute scramble.]*

**BENTLEY:** She's awake! Places everyone!

*[The brothers line up. The trucks line up. The backyard looks AMAZING — even better than planned thanks to Emery's accidental improvements.]*

*[Mom opens the back door. She sees the rainbow bouquet, the banner perfectly held by a nail (and formerly Emery), the rainbow stepping stone path...]*

**MOM:** *(hand over heart)* Oh my goodness...

**ALL THREE BOYS:** SURPRISE! WE LOVE YOU MOM!

**EMERY:** *(waves both arms from her walker, Bella beeping beside her)*

**MOM:** *(tears in her eyes, laughing)* Did YOU help with this, Emery?

*[Emery bounces in her walker. Bella beeps.]*

**BENTLEY:** She may have... reorganized some things.

**JENSEN:** She made a rainbow!

**RUSSELL:** She has a gift.

*[Mom kneels down and hugs all three boys at once. Then she carefully picks up Emery from her walker and holds her close.]*

**MOM:** *(softly)* This is the most beautiful surprise I've ever seen.

*[Emery grabs a yellow flower from the bouquet and tries to put it in Mom's hair. Mom lets her.]*

---

## SCENE 6 — THE CELEBRATION (5:00–5:30)

*[Quick warm montage — the family sitting together in the backyard. Max, Charlie, Remy, Bella, and Dig all parked in a semicircle like they're part of the family too. Foreman Bear shows up with a cake (truck-shaped, of course). Streamers blowing gently. Emery in Mom's lap.]*

*[Bentley catches a baseball by himself, content. Jensen draws in his notebook. Russell is somehow already eating a second piece of cake. Bella sits beside Emery's now-empty walker.]*

---

## SCENE 7 — JENSEN'S MOMENT (5:30–6:15)

**JENSEN:** *(to camera, warm and quiet — different energy from other episodes)*
I learned something really important today for... well, for life.

When you love someone — you SHOW them.
You use colors to make things beautiful.
*(points to rainbow bouquet)*
Red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple — RAINBOW colors!

You use your words — *(points to banner)* WE LOVE YOU.
You use your hands — *(mimes flower in hair)*
And sometimes...

*(looks at Emery who is chewing on a streamer)*

...your baby sister does it best of all.
Because she didn't know she was helping.
She just... wanted to be with us.
And that made everything better.

**BENTLEY:** *(quietly, beside him)* That's the best thing you've ever said, Jensen.

**RUSSELL:** *(mouth full of cake)* ...I also love you, Mom.

**MOM:** *(from her chair, smiling)* I love you all so much.

---

## SCENE 8 — OUTRO (6:15–6:45)

*[Warm sunset light. All characters together. Trucks glow in the golden light.]*

**JENSEN:** *(to camera)* Hey friends — what are the rainbow colors? Say them with me!

*[Colors appear one by one in rainbow order:]*
**JENSEN:** Red... orange... yellow... green... blue... purple!

And most importantly — tell someone YOU LOVE THEM today!

**ALL:** See you next time on Big Truck Adventures!

**RUSSELL:** VROOM VROOM!

**EMERY:** *(pats Mom's face)*

*[Everyone melts. Trucks honk softly. OUTRO SONG plays gently. Subscribe animation.]*

## PRODUCTION NOTES

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Runtime | ~7 minutes |
| Educational skills | Rainbow colors (ROYGBV), feelings/emotions vocabulary, helping others |
| Repeated phrase | "I learned that for kindergarten!" — but THIS episode Jensen says "for life" |
| Viewer interaction | Rainbow colors at outro + tell someone you love them |
| Emery's role | THE STAR — every mishap she causes makes things better. Biggest Emery episode |
| Russell's role | Wrong color order, cake gag, genuine "I love you Mom" moment |
| Bentley's role | Vulnerable — softer than usual, genuinely touched by his siblings |
| Bella's role | Lets Emery out — small guilt, big heart |
| Dig's role | Digs perfect flower pots — shows he's part of the family now |
| Season 1 finale feel | Warm, emotional, brings the whole crew together — families watching will cry happy tears |
| Jensen's monologue | Most mature speech of Season 1 — sets up Season 2 with a deeper emotional tone |
