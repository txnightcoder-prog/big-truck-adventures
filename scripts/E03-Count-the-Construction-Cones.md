# BIG TRUCK ADVENTURES
## Episode 3: "Count the Construction Cones"
**Runtime:** ~7 minutes | **Educational Focus:** Counting 1–20, Following Directions, Left/Right
**Characters:** Bentley, Jensen, Russell, Emery, Mom, Max, Charlie, Remy, Bella

---

## SCENE 1 — INTRO SONG (0:00–0:30)
*[SHORT VERSION theme — this episode has a cold open]*

---

## SCENE 2 — COLD OPEN: THE BLOCKED ROAD (0:00–0:45)

*[A wide shot of Truck Town's main road. It's completely blocked by orange construction cones — dozens of them, in a big winding maze. A sign reads: "ROAD WORK AHEAD."]*

*[Remy the Road Roller zooms up — and screeches to a halt right at the first cone.]*

**REMY:** ROLL WITH IT— *(BONK — hits the first cone)* Oof.

*[The cone wobbles. Remy's eyes go huge. He slowly backs away.]*

**REMY:** *(to camera, whispering)* ...There are a LOT of cones.

*[THEME SONG — SHORT VERSION plays]*

---

## SCENE 3 — THE BRIEFING (0:45–1:30)

*[All four siblings arrive at the edge of the cone maze. Emery's walker bumps the first cone and she giggles. Mom stands nearby with Emery.]*

**BENTLEY:** *(scanning the maze, hands on hips)* Okay. Classic cone situation.

**JENSEN:** How do we get through?

*[A construction worker — a friendly round bear character named FOREMAN BEAR — waddles up with a clipboard.]*

**FOREMAN BEAR:** Mornin' kids! Sorry about the mess. We're fixing the road but we need someone smart to count all the cones and find the safe path through!

**BENTLEY:** Count the cones?

**FOREMAN BEAR:** Every cone has a number on it — one through twenty. Follow them in ORDER and you'll find the safe route. Skip one and you're stuck!

**RUSSELL:** *(already running toward the maze)* I GOT IT! One... *(disappears into the cones)*

*[Clanging sounds. Cones flying everywhere. Russell reappears covered in cones like a hat stack.]*

**RUSSELL:** *(muffled, under cones)* I... may have gotten some out of order.

**JENSEN:** *(to camera)* I can do this! I know how to count to twenty! Let's go!

---

## SCENE 4 — THE COUNTING MAZE (1:30–4:00)

*[Jensen leads the team through the maze, spotting each numbered cone in order. Charlie follows overhead with the crane for aerial spotting.]*

### CONES 1–5
*[Easy section — cones are close together and clearly visible.]*

**JENSEN:** Cone number ONE! *(steps forward)*
Two! Three! Four! FIVE!

*[Big glowing numbers appear above each cone as Jensen counts.]*

**RUSSELL:** *(recovered, running alongside)* I SEE SIX! OVER THERE!

**JENSEN:** Good eyes, Russell! Six!

### CONES 6–10

*[The path splits — two directions. Jensen has to figure out which way to go.]*

**BENTLEY:** Which way? Left or right?

**JENSEN:** Charlie — can you see from up there?

**CHARLIE:** *(from crane height)* I can see cone seven — it's to the LEFT, Jensen!

**JENSEN:** LEFT! *(points left)*

**RUSSELL:** *(already going right)* WAIT — LEFT? But RIGHT looks fun!

**BENTLEY:** Russell. LEFT.

**RUSSELL:** *(turns around, glasses askew)* Left is also fun.

*[Jensen counts seven, eight, nine, ten — each cone glowing as they step.]*

**JENSEN:** TEN! We made it to ten! *(holds up both hands — ten fingers)*
Ten fingers — ten cones! The number TEN!

*[Emery, being pushed by Mom along the safe path edge, claps her little hands.]*

### CONES 11–15

*[Trickier section — cones are spread further apart. The path goes up a small hill.]*

**JENSEN:** Eleven... twelve... *(squints ahead)* Where's thirteen?

**CHARLIE:** *(scanning)* I see it — it rolled a little. It's behind that barrel!

*[Remy zooms behind the barrel and nudges cone 13 back into place.]*

**REMY:** ROLL WITH IT! Thirteen — SAVED!

**JENSEN:** Thirteen! Fourteen! FIFTEEN!

*[Big "15" appears on screen with a halfway celebration — confetti pops.]*

**BENTLEY:** Halfway! You're doing it, Jensen!

### CONES 16–20

*[Final stretch — cones are in a tricky zigzag pattern. Left, right, left, right.]*

**JENSEN:** Sixteen — LEFT! Seventeen — RIGHT! Eighteen — LEFT!

*[The team follows perfectly, zigzagging through the maze.]*

**RUSSELL:** *(loving this)* It's like a DANCE! *(does little dance moves between cones)*

**JENSEN:** Nineteen... *(looks around — no cone visible)*

*[Everyone stops. No cone 19 anywhere.]*

**BENTLEY:** Where's nineteen?

*[Emery suddenly starts banging on her walker tray excitedly, pointing toward a bush.]*

**MOM:** Emery — what do you see?

*[Emery keeps pointing. Everyone looks. Cone 19 is lying in the bush — it had blown over in the wind.]*

**JENSEN:** EMERY FOUND IT! She spotted cone nineteen!

*[He runs over, sets it upright.]*

**JENSEN:** Nineteen! And now... *(dramatic pause, looks ahead)* ...TWENTY!

*[A giant glowing "20" explodes on screen. The whole maze lights up. The safe path glows green. Foreman Bear does a happy dance.]*

---

## SCENE 5 — THROUGH THE MAZE (4:00–5:00)

*[The whole team walks the glowing safe path triumphantly. Trucks roll through. Emery's walker goes through last — Bella rolling right beside her.]*

**FOREMAN BEAR:** You did it! You counted all twenty cones and found the safe path! The road is OPEN!

*[MAX drives through first, dump bed raised in celebration. CHARLIE follows, crane arm up like a victory wave. REMY rolls through doing little spins. BELLA putters through slowly — Emery waves from her walker.]*

**BENTLEY:** *(fist pump)* That's what I'm talking about!

**RUSSELL:** *(running through)* VROOM VROOM! ROLL WITH IT! LEFT RIGHT LEFT RIGHT!

---

## SCENE 6 — JENSEN'S MOMENT (5:00–6:00)

*[Jensen stands at the end of the maze, facing camera. Numbers 1–20 float around him.]*

**JENSEN:** I learned SO MUCH today for kindergarten!
Counting goes from ONE... all the way to TWENTY!
*(counts on fingers, runs out of fingers, keeps going on toes)*
One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten...
Eleven, twelve, thirteen, fourteen, fifteen...
Sixteen, seventeen, eighteen, nineteen... TWENTY!

And when you follow the numbers IN ORDER — you can find the right path!

And LEFT *(points left)* and RIGHT *(points right)* help you know which way to go!

**BENTLEY:** And if you get stuck?

**JENSEN:** Look for clues! *(looks at Emery)* ...Or let your baby sister point at things.

**EMERY:** *(triumphant)* Ba-ba-BRRM!

*[Everyone laughs.]*

---

## SCENE 7 — VIEWER INTERACTION (6:00–6:30)

**JENSEN:** *(to camera)* Hey friends — can YOU count to twenty with me? Ready?

*[Slow, clear, with each number appearing on screen big and bold:]*

**JENSEN:** One... two... three... four... five...
Six... seven... eight... nine... TEN!
Eleven... twelve... thirteen... fourteen... fifteen...
Sixteen... seventeen... eighteen... nineteen... TWENTY!

*(big celebration)* YOU DID IT! You counted all the way to TWENTY!
