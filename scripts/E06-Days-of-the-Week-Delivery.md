# BIG TRUCK ADVENTURES
## Episode 6: "The Days of the Week Delivery"
**Runtime:** ~7 minutes | **Educational Focus:** Days of the Week, Sequencing, Yesterday/Today/Tomorrow
**Characters:** Bentley, Jensen, Russell, Emery, Mom, Max, Charlie, Remy, Bella, Dig, Foreman Bear

---

## SCENE 1 — INTRO SONG (0:00–0:30)
*[Full theme song — now includes DIG in the truck lineup!]*

---

## SCENE 2 — THE DELIVERY PUZZLE (0:30–1:15)

*[Monday morning. The truck yard. Seven delivery boxes are lined up, each one labeled with a day of the week — but they're all in the WRONG order. Foreman Bear looks frazzled.]*

**FOREMAN BEAR:** Oh no oh no oh no...

**JENSEN:** *(arriving with backpack)* Foreman Bear! What happened?

**FOREMAN BEAR:** The weekly supply delivery came in — but the boxes got all mixed up! Each box has supplies for a DIFFERENT day of the week, and they have to be delivered in the right order! Monday first, then Tuesday, then Wednesday...

**JENSEN:** Days of the week! I know days of the week! I've been learning them for kindergarten!

**BENTLEY:** What are the days again?

**JENSEN:** *(counting on fingers)*
Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday!
Seven days — one whole week!

**FOREMAN BEAR:** That's right! Can you put the boxes in order and help the trucks deliver them?

**JENSEN:** WE CAN DO IT! *(backpack bounces as he jumps)*

*[Emery in her walker rolls over and pats the Monday box approvingly.]*

---

## SCENE 3 — FINDING THE ORDER (1:15–2:30)

*[Seven boxes scattered around the yard — each has a day written on it in big colorful letters.]*

**JENSEN:** Okay — Monday is FIRST! That's today! Which box says Monday?

**RUSSELL:** *(scanning)* M-O-N... MONDAY! Over here!

*[Russell drags the Monday box to the front. He strains — it's heavy.]*

**RUSSELL:** *(straining)* Why... is... Monday... so... heavy?

**BENTLEY:** *(easily lifts it)* Because you're three, Russell.

**RUSSELL:** *(immediately)* Three is a number.

*[Max loads the Monday box into his dump bed.]*

**JENSEN:** Monday is day ONE of the week! After Monday comes...?

**ALL:** TUESDAY!

**JENSEN:** *(finds Tuesday box)* Tuesday is day TWO! *(holds up 2 fingers)*

*[Charlie lifts the Tuesday box with his crane, gently.]*

**JENSEN:** After Tuesday comes...?

**RUSSELL:** *(confident)* WEDNESDAY! W-W-W... Wednesday starts with W! Like WHOOSH! *(makes whoosh sound, spins)*

**JENSEN:** That's right! Wednesday is day THREE! *(holds up 3 fingers)*

---

## SCENE 4 — THE TRICKY MIDDLE (2:30–3:30)

*[Wednesday, Thursday, Friday boxes — the middle of the week.]*

**JENSEN:** After Wednesday comes THURSDAY — day FOUR!
And after Thursday comes FRIDAY — day FIVE!

**BENTLEY:** Friday — that's the last school day of the week!

**JENSEN:** *(excitedly)* And when I start kindergarten — Friday will be the end of my school WEEK!

**RUSSELL:** What's in the Friday box?

*[They peek inside — it's full of little party horns and confetti.]*

**RUSSELL:** *(gasps)* Friday has a PARTY in it?!

**FOREMAN BEAR:** Friday is when the whole crew celebrates the week's work!

**RUSSELL:** *(grabs a party horn, blows it)* FRIDAY IS MY FAVORITE DAY!

**BENTLEY:** It's Monday, Russell.

**RUSSELL:** Monday is my SECOND favorite day.

*[Emery blows a tiny party horn too — nobody knows where she got it. Mom appears in the background looking for it.]*

---

## SCENE 5 — THE WEEKEND (3:30–4:30)

*[Two boxes left — Saturday and Sunday.]*

**JENSEN:** After Friday comes SATURDAY — day SIX! That's the weekend!

**BENTLEY:** Saturday — game day!

**JENSEN:** And after Saturday comes SUNDAY — day SEVEN! The last day of the week!

**RUSSELL:** And then it starts over with Monday again?

**JENSEN:** Yep! Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday... and then MONDAY again! It goes around and around like a wheel!

**REMY:** *(spinning in a circle)* LIKE A ROAD ROLLER WHEEL! ROLL WITH IT!

**DIG:** *(scooping the Sunday box gently into his bucket)* I've got Sunday!

*[All seven boxes are now in order, loaded onto the trucks.]*

**FOREMAN BEAR:** *(tearfully happy)* They're in order! Monday through Sunday — perfect! You did it!

---

## SCENE 6 — THE DELIVERY PARADE (4:30–5:15)

*[Quick fun montage — the trucks deliver each box to a different part of town in order.]*

- **Monday box** → Max delivers to the school (pencils and supplies for the week)
- **Tuesday box** → Charlie delivers to the library (books!)
- **Wednesday box** → Remy delivers to the park (playground equipment)
- **Thursday box** → Dig delivers to the fire station (new hoses)
- **Friday box** → Bella delivers to the community center (party supplies — confetti flies!)
- **Saturday box** → Max delivers to the baseball field (Bentley pumps his fist)
- **Sunday box** → All trucks together deliver to the families — it's full of picnic baskets

*[The whole town celebrates. Emery in her walker leads a tiny parade down the sidewalk, Bella rolling beside her.]*

---

## SCENE 7 — JENSEN'S MOMENT (5:15–6:00)

**JENSEN:** *(to camera, seven colorful day-banners behind him)*
I learned SO MUCH today for kindergarten!

There are SEVEN days in a week — and they always go in the same order!
Let's say them together — ready?

*(points to each banner)*
MONDAY — day 1!
TUESDAY — day 2!
WEDNESDAY — day 3!
THURSDAY — day 4!
FRIDAY — day 5!
SATURDAY — day 6!
SUNDAY — day 7!

And then it starts all over again with Monday!

**RUSSELL:** *(appearing beside Jensen)* My favorite is Friday because of the party horns.

**JENSEN:** Every day is special, Russell.

**RUSSELL:** *(nodding solemnly)* ...But Friday has horns.

*[Emery honks a tiny horn. Everyone jumps. Then everyone laughs.]*

---

## SCENE 8 — OUTRO (6:00–6:30)

**JENSEN:** *(to camera)* Hey friends — can YOU say all seven days with me?

*[Slow and clear, each day pops on screen:]*
**JENSEN:** Monday... Tuesday... Wednesday... Thursday... Friday... Saturday... Sunday!

*[Confetti falls.]*

**ALL:** See you next time on Big Truck Adventures!

**RUSSELL:** VROOM VROOM!

**EMERY:** *(honks tiny horn again)* Ba-ba-BRRM!

*[OUTRO SONG. Subscribe animation. Preview: "Next time — Emery's Big Surprise!"]*

| Element | Detail |

| Russell's role | Discovers Friday box has party supplies — "Friday is my favorite day" gag |
| Bentley's role | Saturday = game day connection — links days to real life |
| Dig's role | First full episode as crew member — delivers Sunday box |
| Remy's road roller spin | "Like a wheel — ROLL WITH IT!" ties the cyclical week concept to his shape |
| Delivery parade | Montage keeps energy high — town community building feel |
