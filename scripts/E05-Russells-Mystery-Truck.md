# BIG TRUCK ADVENTURES
## Episode 5: "Russell's Mystery Truck"
**Runtime:** ~7 minutes | **Educational Focus:** Colors, Shapes, Matching, Process of Elimination
**Characters:** Bentley, Jensen, Russell, Emery, Mom, Max, Charlie, Remy, Bella, Foreman Bear

---

## SCENE 1 — INTRO SONG (0:00–0:30)
*[Full theme song]*

---

## SCENE 2 — THE MYSTERY ARRIVAL (0:30–1:15)

*[The truck yard. All four trucks are lined up. Russell is in his usual spot — sitting on Remy's bumper, studying a truck magazine. His glasses are slightly crooked.]*

*[Suddenly a LOUD horn blast. A huge truck covered in a tarp rolls into the yard. Nobody can see what kind it is.]*

**RUSSELL:** *(leaps up, magazine flying)* A NEW TRUCK! NEW TRUCK IN THE YARD!

*[He sprints toward it. Bentley grabs his collar.]*

**BENTLEY:** Russell — WAIT.

**RUSSELL:** But — NEW TRUCK!

**BENTLEY:** We don't know anything about it yet!

*[Foreman Bear waddles out from behind the mystery truck, clipboard in hand.]*

**FOREMAN BEAR:** Morning, kids! This truck is joining our crew — but before we introduce it officially, I thought Russell might like to figure out what it is himself!

**RUSSELL:** *(gasps so hard his glasses fly off)* A MYSTERY TRUCK?! For ME to solve?!

**FOREMAN BEAR:** I'll give you clues — colors, shapes, and what it does. Can you figure it out?

**RUSSELL:** *(picks up glasses, puts them on upside down, corrects them)* I know EVERY truck. Let's GO.

---

## SCENE 3 — CLUE 1: COLOR (1:15–2:15)

**FOREMAN BEAR:** Clue number one — the mystery truck is YELLOW.

*[Russell immediately spins around to the other trucks.]*

**RUSSELL:** YELLOW! Yellow trucks... yellow trucks... *(counting on fingers)* Cement mixers are sometimes yellow... School buses are yellow... EXCAVATORS are yellow!

**JENSEN:** How many yellow trucks could it be?

**RUSSELL:** *(thinking hard)* Could be... a lot. I need more clues.

*[Jensen pulls out a little notebook.]*

**JENSEN:** I'll write it down! Clue one: YELLOW. *(writes a big yellow Y)*

**BENTLEY:** Good thinking, Jensen. Russell — what other yellow trucks do you know?

**RUSSELL:** Bulldozers! Front loaders! Graders! Forklifts! Tower cranes are sometimes yellow!

**EMERY:** *(banging walker tray)* Ba-ba-BRRM!

**RUSSELL:** *(to Emery)* I KNOW, Emery, there are SO MANY yellow trucks!

---

## SCENE 4 — CLUE 2: SHAPE (2:15–3:15)

**FOREMAN BEAR:** Clue number two — the mystery truck has a long RECTANGLE arm that reaches up HIGH.

**RUSSELL:** *(freezes)* A long... rectangle arm... that goes HIGH...

**JENSEN:** Rectangle! Four sides — two long, two short! *(draws it in notebook)*

**RUSSELL:** A long rectangle arm that goes up... *(eyes go wide behind glasses)* That narrows it down A LOT. Could be a crane... could be a boom lift... could be a—

**BENTLEY:** How many are left?

**RUSSELL:** *(counting fingers)* Maybe... four or five types? Still need one more clue!

**JENSEN:** *(reading notebook)* Yellow... rectangle arm... reaches HIGH...

**REMY:** *(rolling excitedly)* OOH OOH I THINK I KNOW!

**BENTLEY:** Remy, shh! Let Russell figure it out!

**REMY:** *(zips it, but is visibly vibrating with excitement)*

---

## SCENE 5 — CLUE 3: WHAT IT DOES (3:15–4:15)

**FOREMAN BEAR:** Clue number three — and this is the last one. This truck digs into the GROUND. Its rectangle arm goes DOWN as well as UP. And it has a big SCOOP at the end that looks like... a C shape.

*[Long pause. Russell's eyes go huge behind his glasses. His mouth slowly opens.]*

**JENSEN:** C shape... like the letter C?

**RUSSELL:** *(barely breathing)* Yellow... long rectangle arm... digs DOWN and UP... big C-shaped scoop...

*[Russell slowly turns toward the covered truck. He walks up to it. He reaches out... and pulls off one corner of the tarp, just enough to see the scoop.]*

*[It IS a C shape. Yellow. Massive.]*

**RUSSELL:** *(turns to camera, completely serious)*
...It's an EXCAVATOR.

*[He grabs the full tarp and yanks it off in one dramatic pull. Revealed: a gleaming yellow excavator with a huge C-shaped bucket, arm fully extended.]*

**EVERYONE:** WHOOOOOA!

**RUSSELL:** *(jumping, spinning, glasses flying)* EXCAVATOR! I KNEW IT! IT'S AN EXCAVATOR! A YELLOW EXCAVATOR WITH A C-SHAPED BUCKET AND A RECTANGLE ARM AND I KNEW IT I KNEW IT I KNEW IT!

**EMERY:** *(wild baby joy)* BA-BA-BRRM BA-BA-BRRM!

---

## SCENE 6 — MEETING THE NEW TRUCK (4:15–5:00)

*[The excavator's cab swings toward the group. Its face — friendly, big round eyes, a wide grin.]*

**EXCAVATOR:** *(deep but cheerful voice)* The name's DIG! Nice to meet you all!

**RUSSELL:** *(breathless)* Your name is DIG. You're a yellow excavator. Your arm is a rectangle and your bucket is a C. You are the most beautiful truck I have ever seen.

**DIG:** *(laughing)* I heard you're the truck expert around here.

**RUSSELL:** *(standing very straight, glasses perfectly centered for once)* I am.

**BENTLEY:** Welcome to the crew, Dig.

**JENSEN:** *(in notebook)* New crew member: DIG. Yellow excavator. Rectangle arm. C-shaped bucket. *(draws a little truck)*

---

## SCENE 7 — JENSEN'S MOMENT (5:00–5:45)

**JENSEN:** *(to camera)*
I learned SO MUCH today for kindergarten!

When you don't know something right away — you use CLUES!
Clue one: COLOR — yellow! *(yellow splash)*
Clue two: SHAPE — a long rectangle arm! *(rectangle appears)*
Clue three: WHAT IT DOES — digs, with a C-shaped scoop! *(C appears)*

Put the clues TOGETHER — and you can figure out ANYTHING!
That's called... *(thinks very hard)* ...DETECTIVE THINKING!

**RUSSELL:** *(tapping temple)* It's all up here.

**BENTLEY:** You literally didn't know what it was until the third clue.

**RUSSELL:** ...The third clue was very helpful.

*[Everyone laughs.]*

---

## SCENE 8 — OUTRO (5:45–6:15)

**JENSEN:** *(to camera)* Hey friends — what color was the mystery truck?

*[Pause. Yellow flash.]*

**JENSEN:** YELLOW! And what shape was its arm?

*[Pause. Rectangle flash.]*

**JENSEN:** RECTANGLE! You're so smart!

**ALL:** See you next time on Big Truck Adventures!

**RUSSELL:** VROOM VROOM!

**EMERY:** Ba-ba-BRRM!

*[DIG adds a new sound — a deep CLANKING scoop sound. Everyone laughs.]*

*[OUTRO SONG. Subscribe animation. Preview: "Next time — The Days of the Week Delivery!"]*

| Runtime | ~7 minutes |
| Educational skills | Colors, shapes (rectangle, C-shape/curve), process of elimination, logical deduction |
| Repeated phrase | "I learned that for kindergarten!" |
| Viewer interaction | Answer color and shape questions at outro |
| New character | DIG the Yellow Excavator — joins the permanent crew |
| Russell's role | This is HIS episode — he leads the whole investigation |
| Jensen's role | Takes notes in a notebook — reinforces writing/letter skills subtly |
| Emery's role | Reacts to Russell's excitement with matching energy — they mirror each other |
| Remy's role | Knows the answer but is told to shh — vibrates with excitement |
| Running gag | Russell's glasses fly off at peak excitement moments — 3 times this episode |
