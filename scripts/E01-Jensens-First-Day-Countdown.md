# BIG TRUCK ADVENTURES
## Episode 1: "Jensen's First Day Countdown"
**Runtime:** ~7 minutes | **Educational Focus:** Letters, Numbers 1–10, Colors
**Characters:** Bentley, Jensen, Russell, Emery, Mom, Max, Charlie, Remy, Bella

---

## SCENE 1 — INTRO SONG (0:00–0:30)

*[UPBEAT THEME MUSIC — truck engine revs, horn honks]*

**ALL CHARACTERS (singing):**
> Big Truck Adventures, here we go!
> Letters and numbers, let's put on a show!
> Bentley, Jensen, Russell, and Emery too —
> Big Truck Adventures — just me and you!

*[Trucks drive across screen. Each character waves. Baby Emery bounces and goes "Ba-ba-BRRM!"]*

---

## SCENE 2 — THE KITCHEN (0:30–1:15)

*[Morning. Bright sunny kitchen. Mom is making breakfast. Emery is in her high chair banging a spoon. Jensen runs in wearing a backpack way too big for him.]*

**JENSEN:** Mom! Mom! Mom! Guess what day it is?!

**MOM:** *(smiling)* Hmm... is it... Tuesday?

**JENSEN:** NOOO! It's my kindergarten countdown day! Only TEN more days until my first day of school!

**MOM:** Ten days! That's so exciting, Jensen!

**EMERY:** Ba-ba-BRRM!

**JENSEN:** See? Even Emery is excited!

*[Bentley walks in, baseball cap on, eating an apple.]*

**BENTLEY:** Ten days? That's not very long. You better make sure you have everything you need.

**JENSEN:** *(gasps)* Everything I need... My list! I need my school list!

*[Jensen pulls a crumpled piece of paper from his backpack. He tries to read it but holds it upside down.]*

**JENSEN:** Uh... I need... a... hmm.

**BENTLEY:** *(peeks over)* Dude. It's upside down.

**JENSEN:** *(flips it)* Oh! I knew that.

*[Russell zooms in making truck sounds, arms out like an airplane.]*

**RUSSELL:** VROOM VROOM! Russell is HERE! What are we doing?!

**JENSEN:** We're going on a supply run! I need everything on my kindergarten list — and I have TEN days to get it all!

**BENTLEY:** Alright team... let's build a plan!

---

## SCENE 3 — THE TRUCK YARD (1:15–2:00)

*[The brothers run outside to the truck yard. Max, Charlie, Remy, and Bella are all lined up waiting. Emery is in Mom's arms watching from the porch.]*

**RUSSELL:** *(pointing excitedly)* That's Max the Dump Truck! And Charlie the Crane! And Remy the Road Roller! And little Bella the Baby Loader!

**CHARLIE:** *(warm, friendly voice)* Good morning, Jensen! Ready for your supply run?

**JENSEN:** Ready! But Charlie — I have TEN things on my list. Can you help me count them?

**CHARLIE:** Of course! Let's count together!

*[Jensen holds up the list. Each item glows as they count.]*

**JENSEN + CHARLIE:** One... two... three... four... five... six... seven... eight... nine... TEN!

**JENSEN:** TEN things! That's the number TEN!

*[A big glowing "10" bounces on screen.]*

**RUSSELL:** *(jumping)* I can count to ten TOO! One, two, skip a few, TEN!

**BENTLEY:** *(facepalm)* That's... not how counting works, Russell.

**EMERY:** *(from porch, giggling)* Ba-ba!

---

## SCENE 4 — THE SUPPLY HUNT (2:00–4:30)

*[The team sets off through the colorful town. Each supply is found by solving a letter, color, or number clue.]*

### STOP 1 — THE PENCIL SHOP

*[A shop with a giant pencil sign. The door has a letter "P" on it.]*

**JENSEN:** We need pencils! And look — the shop has the letter P on the door!

**BENTLEY:** P for pencils!

**JENSEN:** I learned that for kindergarten! P makes the "puh" sound — P-P-Pencil!

*[Max the Dump Truck backs up to the shop. The pencils are loaded into Max's bed.]*

**MAX:** *(deep, rumbling voice)* Pencils loaded! What's next?

### STOP 2 — THE CRAYON FACTORY

*[A rainbow-colored building. Crayons in every color come rolling out on a conveyor belt.]*

**RUSSELL:** WOAH! Colors! So many COLORS!

**JENSEN:** I need one of EACH color! But which ones? *(reads list)* Red... blue... yellow... green!

**RUSSELL:** *(zooming around)* I see RED! I see BLUE! I see... I see... *(trips over a yellow crayon)* ...yellow! I found it by falling on it!

*[Everyone laughs. Emery, watching from Charlie's cab window, claps her tiny hands.]*

**JENSEN:** *(holding up each crayon)* Red, blue, yellow, green — FOUR colors! The number four!

*[Big glowing "4" bounces on screen.]*

### STOP 3 — THE BACKPACK BRIDGE

*[A bridge with backpacks hanging from it. Each backpack has a number tag.]*

**CHARLIE:** Jensen, your list says you need backpack number FIVE.

**JENSEN:** Number five! Let me find it!

*[Jensen walks along the bridge counting the backpacks.]*

**JENSEN:** One... two... three... four... FIVE! *(grabs the blue backpack)* I learned that for kindergarten — you always count to find the right one!

**BENTLEY:** *(nodding, impressed)* Nice work, little bro.

### STOP 4 — REMY'S ROAD

*[A long road with letter signs on both sides — like a road lined with alphabet flags.]*

**REMY:** *(rolling alongside)* Jensen, the scissors shop is at the end of the alphabet road — but to get there you have to name each letter you pass!

**JENSEN:** I can do that! A... B... C... D... E...

*[All characters join in as they drive down the road.]*

**ALL:** ...F... G... H... I... J...

**RUSSELL:** *(confidently wrong)* ...K... L... M... N... O... POTATO!

**BENTLEY:** Russell. It's P.

**RUSSELL:** P-Potato?

**JENSEN:** P! Just P! P for pencil! P for parade! P for... *(thinks hard)* ...P for PROUD! Because I'm proud of my letters!

*[The scissors shop appears at the end of the road.]*

---

## SCENE 5 — THE PROBLEM (4:30–5:15)

*[The team has 9 of the 10 items. They stop in the town square. Bella the Baby Loader putters up next to Emery's stroller.]*

**JENSEN:** *(counting supplies)* One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine... *(looks at list)* We're missing number TEN!

**BENTLEY:** What's number ten on the list?

**JENSEN:** *(reads slowly)* A... a... *(squints)* ...a NAME TAG. A name tag with my name on it!

**RUSSELL:** What does your name start with?

**JENSEN:** J! J for Jensen! It makes the "juh" sound!

**BENTLEY:** So we need to find something with the letter J...

*[Emery, sitting in her stroller next to Bella, suddenly reaches out and grabs something shiny from Bella's tiny loader bucket. She waves it around, giggling.]*

**RUSSELL:** EMERY HAS SOMETHING!

*[Everyone looks. It's a shiny name tag blank — the perfect size for Jensen's name.]*

**JENSEN:** *(gasps)* Bella was carrying it this whole time! Emery found it!

**MOM:** *(walking up smiling)* Looks like your little sister had your back, Jensen.

**EMERY:** Ba-ba-BRRM!

*[Everyone laughs and cheers.]*

---

## SCENE 6 — JENSEN'S MOMENT (5:15–6:30)

*[Back home. Jensen lays out all TEN supplies on the kitchen table. He counts them one more time, touching each one.]*

**JENSEN:** One pencil... two crayons — wait, FOUR crayons... *(adjusts)* One box of four crayons... one backpack... one pair of scissors... *(keeps counting)* ...and my name tag! TEN things! All TEN!

*[A big glowing "10" appears again.]*

**JENSEN:** *(turns to camera, big smile)*
I learned SO MUCH today for kindergarten!
The letter P is for pencil!
The letter J is for Jensen!
Red, blue, yellow, and green are four colors — and four is the number FOUR!
And when you count to ten... *(counts on fingers)* ...one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, TEN — you can find ANYTHING!

**BENTLEY:** *(arm around Jensen)* You're gonna be the best kindergartener ever.

**RUSSELL:** Can I come to kindergarten too?

**BENTLEY:** You're three, Russell.

**RUSSELL:** *(pause)* ...Three is a number.

*[Everyone laughs.]*

**EMERY:** *(grabs the name tag and waves it)*

**JENSEN:** *(gently takes it back)* That's mine, Emery. But I'll make you one too someday.

*[Mom watches from the doorway, smiling.]*

---

## SCENE 7 — OUTRO (6:30–7:00)

*[All characters gather in the truck yard at sunset. Trucks honk. Emery waves.]*

**JENSEN:** *(to camera)* Hey friends! Can YOU count to ten with me?

*[Pause for viewer response.]*

**JENSEN:** One... two... three... four... five... six... seven... eight... nine... TEN! You did it!

**ALL:** See you next time on Big Truck Adventures!

**RUSSELL:** VROOM VROOM!

**EMERY:** Ba-ba-BRRM!

*[THEME MUSIC plays out. Subscribe animation appears.]*
