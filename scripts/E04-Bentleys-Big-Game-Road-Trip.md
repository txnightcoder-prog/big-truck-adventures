# BIG TRUCK ADVENTURES
## Episode 4: "Bentley's Big Game Road Trip"
**Runtime:** ~7 minutes | **Educational Focus:** Shapes, Road Signs, Left/Right Directions, Maps
**Characters:** Bentley, Jensen, Russell, Emery, Mom, Max, Charlie, Remy, Bella, Foreman Bear

---

## SCENE 1 — INTRO SONG (0:00–0:30)
*[Full theme song — trucks parade across screen, all 4 siblings wave]*

---

## SCENE 2 — THE BIG GAME MORNING (0:30–1:15)

*[Bentley's bedroom. He wakes up, shoots out of bed, grabs his baseball glove and hat in one motion.]*

**BENTLEY:** GAME DAY! GAME DAY! GAME DAY!

*[He slides down the hallway in his socks, bursts into the kitchen. Mom is feeding Emery in her high chair. Emery has oatmeal on her face.]*

**MOM:** Good morning, Bentley! Big game today!

**BENTLEY:** Coach says if we win today we get the CHAMPIONSHIP TROPHY! *(holds up glove like a trophy)*

**EMERY:** *(slaps oatmeal tray excitedly)* Ba-ba-BRRM!

*[Jensen and Russell run in already dressed.]*

**JENSEN:** We're all coming to watch!

**RUSSELL:** *(wearing Bentley's old baseball cap — way too big, covers his eyes)* I am READY. I can't see anything but I am READY.

**BENTLEY:** *(laughing, adjusts Russell's cap)* Alright team — let's roll. Max is waiting outside.

---

## SCENE 3 — THE ROAD PROBLEM (1:15–2:00)

*[Outside. Max the Dump Truck is revved up and ready. But the usual road to the baseball field is blocked — a big detour sign with arrows pointing three different directions.]*

**MAX:** *(rumbling voice)* Road's blocked, Bentley. Three detour routes. Don't know which one leads to the field.

**BENTLEY:** *(stares at the three paths)* Hmm. Which way do we go?

*[Charlie the Crane Truck rolls up with a MAP in his crane arm.]*

**CHARLIE:** Good morning! I brought the town map — but to read it, you need to know your shapes. Every road sign has a shape that tells you what to do!

**JENSEN:** *(eyes lighting up)* Shapes! I know shapes!

**BENTLEY:** Then you're on navigation duty, Jensen. Let's move — game starts in one hour!

---

## SCENE 4 — THE SHAPE ROAD (2:00–4:30)

*[The team sets off down the first detour. Road signs line the path — each one a different shape.]*

### STOP 1 — THE TRIANGLE SIGN

*[A bright yellow triangle sign with an exclamation point.]*

**JENSEN:** Triangle! A triangle has THREE sides — one, two, three! *(traces it with finger)*
Triangle signs mean WARNING — something's coming up ahead!

**REMY:** *(rolling up)* What's the warning?

*[They round the corner — a big muddy puddle covers the road.]*

**RUSSELL:** *(already running toward it)* MUDDY PUDDLE —

**BENTLEY + JENSEN:** RUSSELL NO!

*[Russell freezes one foot in the air. His glasses are fogged from excitement.]*

**RUSSELL:** *(whispering)* ...I almost did it again.

**MAX:** *(navigating carefully around the edge)* Triangle warned us. Good eye, Jensen.

### STOP 2 — THE SQUARE SIGN

*[A blue square sign with an arrow pointing right.]*

**JENSEN:** Square! A square has FOUR sides — all the same length! One, two, three, four!
Square signs are information signs — this one says go RIGHT!

**BENTLEY:** Right it is! *(points right)* Max — right turn!

**MAX:** Roger that.

*[The whole convoy turns right. Remy overshoots the turn slightly.]*

**REMY:** ROLL WITH IT! *(spins back into line)*

### STOP 3 — THE CIRCLE SIGN

*[A red circle sign — a stop sign.]*

**JENSEN:** Circle! A circle has NO sides — it's perfectly round, round, round!
Red circle means STOP!

**BENTLEY:** Stop? Why do we need to stop here?

*[They stop. A family of ducks waddles slowly across the road. Baby ducks, one by one.]*

**RUSSELL:** *(whispering, enchanted)* ...Ducks.

**EMERY:** *(in Bella's loader bucket beside the road, reaches toward the ducks)* Ba... ba...

*[Last duck crosses. Road is clear.]*

**JENSEN:** Circle said stop — and it was RIGHT! We didn't squish any ducks!

**BENTLEY:** Good call, Jensen.

### STOP 4 — THE RECTANGLE SIGN

*[A green rectangle sign — like a highway sign — with the baseball field name and an arrow pointing left.]*

**JENSEN:** Rectangle! A rectangle has FOUR sides — but TWO are longer than the other two!
Green rectangle means GO — we're on the right path! And it says... *(reads slowly)* ...Championship Field — LEFT!

**BENTLEY:** *(pumping fist)* LEFT! We're almost there! Jensen — you're incredible!

**JENSEN:** *(beaming)* I learned that for kindergarten!

---

## SCENE 5 — ALMOST THERE (4:30–5:15)

*[The convoy is on the final stretch. The baseball field is visible in the distance. But there's one more obstacle — the road splits into a diamond-shaped roundabout.]*

**BENTLEY:** A roundabout. Which exit do we take?

*[A diamond-shaped sign in the center of the roundabout.]*

**JENSEN:** Diamond! A diamond is like a square... tilted on its corner! Four sides, but pointy on top and bottom!

*[Jensen studies the diamond sign — it has an arrow pointing to exit number 2.]*

**JENSEN:** Exit TWO — that's the second one! Count with me — one... TWO! That one!

*[They take exit two. The baseball field gate appears right in front of them.]*

**BENTLEY:** *(huge grin)* WE MADE IT!

*[Max honks loud. Remy spins in a circle. Charlie raises his crane arm. Bella beeps softly. Emery claps.]*

---

## SCENE 6 — THE GAME (5:15–5:45)

*[Quick montage — Bentley up to bat, focused. He swings. CRACK — the ball flies. His team cheers. Final score: WIN! Bentley holds up the Championship Trophy. The whole family and all four trucks celebrate.]*

**RUSSELL:** *(jumping)* BENTLEY DID IT! BENTLEY DID IT!

**EMERY:** *(waving her arms in the air)* Ba-ba-BRRM!

**MOM:** *(hugging Bentley)* I'm so proud of you!

**BENTLEY:** *(looking at Jensen)* Couldn't have done it without the world's best navigator.

---

## SCENE 7 — JENSEN'S MOMENT (5:45–6:30)

**JENSEN:** *(to camera, shapes appearing around him)*
I learned SO MUCH today for kindergarten!

A TRIANGLE has three sides — and it means WARNING! *(triangle appears)*
A SQUARE has four equal sides — it gives information! *(square appears)*
A CIRCLE has NO sides — round, round, round! *(circle rolls)*
A RECTANGLE has four sides — two long, two short! *(rectangle stretches)*
And a DIAMOND is like a square standing on its tip! *(diamond tips)*

When you know your shapes — you can read signs, find your way, and get to the CHAMPIONSHIP!

**BENTLEY:** *(arm around Jensen)* And win the trophy.

**RUSSELL:** *(holding a sandwich shaped like a triangle)* This sandwich is a TRIANGLE.

**BENTLEY:** Why is your sandwich a triangle?

**RUSSELL:** Because Mom cut it that way and triangles are my favorite shape now.

*[Everyone laughs.]*

---

## SCENE 8 — OUTRO (6:30–7:00)

*[All characters wave. Bentley holds the trophy. Trucks honk.]*

**JENSEN:** *(to camera)* Hey friends — what shapes did we learn today? Can you name them?

*[Pause — shapes appear one by one: triangle, square, circle, rectangle, diamond.]*

**ALL:** See you next time on Big Truck Adventures!

**RUSSELL:** VROOM VROOM!

**EMERY:** Ba-ba-BRRM!

*[OUTRO SONG. Subscribe animation. Preview: "Next time — Russell's Mystery Truck!"]*
