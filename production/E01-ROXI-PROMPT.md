## HOW TO USE THIS IN ROXI.AI

1. Go to roxi.ai and start a new video project
2. Select **"Kids Cartoon"** or **"Animated Story"** style
3. Paste each SCENE PROMPT one at a time — or paste the FULL SCRIPT PROMPT at the bottom
4. Upload character reference images (generated from the Gemini prompts in the companion file)
5. Set video length to **7 minutes**
6. Export as MP4 — upload to YouTube Studio

---

## STYLE SETTINGS (set these first in Roxi)

- **Animation Style:** Bright 2D cartoon, thick outlines, flat colors — similar to Blippi or Cocomelon
- **Color Palette:** Sky blue backgrounds, bright yellows, oranges, greens — very saturated and cheerful
- **Character Style:** Cute rounded proportions, large expressive eyes, simple clean shapes
- **Setting:** Colorful cartoon construction town — blue sky, green grass, orange dirt roads, buildings with bright colors
- **Tone:** Fun, energetic, warm — never scary or dark
- **Target Age:** 2–6 years old

## FULL EPISODE PROMPT (paste this as one block if Roxi supports long-form)

```
Create a 7-minute kids cartoon episode called "Jensen's First Day Countdown" 
for the show "Big Truck Adventures" aimed at children ages 2-6.

CHARACTERS:
- Bentley: age 6, blonde hair, blue eyes, blue baseball jersey and cap, confident leader
- Jensen: age 4, light brown hair, tall and skinny, very expressive face, yellow shirt, big backpack
- Russell: age 3, blonde hair, blue eyes, round glasses always sliding down, green shirt, small and energetic
- Emery: 8 months old, blonde wispy hair, pink onesie, round baby walker, always happy

TRUCKS:
- Max: big blue dump truck, calm confident face
- Charlie: tall amber crane truck, wise warm eyes  
- Remy: short round green road roller, huge excited grin
- Bella: tiny pink baby loader, shy gentle eyes, always next to Emery

STYLE: Bright 2D kids cartoon, thick outlines, flat saturated colors, 
similar to Cocomelon or Blippi. Colorful construction town setting.

STORY: Jensen is excited — only 10 days until kindergarten! 
He needs 10 school supplies. The team goes on a supply hunt through town.
At each stop they learn: the letter P (pencil shop), 
four colors red/blue/yellow/green (crayon factory), 
the number 5 (backpack bridge), and the alphabet A-Z (alphabet road).
Baby Emery accidentally finds the missing 10th item — a name tag — in Bella's loader bucket.
Jensen recaps everything he learned. Everyone counts to 10 together at the end.

EDUCATIONAL CONTENT TO SHOW ON SCREEN:
- Letter P with the word PENCIL
- Letter J with the word JENSEN  
- Colors: RED, BLUE, YELLOW, GREEN (each in their own color)
- Numbers 1 through 10 (counting sequence)
- "I learned that for kindergarten!" catchphrase

END CARD: Subscribe button + preview of next episode.
```
